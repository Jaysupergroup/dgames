What Is Aviatrix?

Aviatrix is an online crash-style casino game based on short, multiplier-driven rounds. During each round, an aircraft takes off while the displayed multiplier increases. The aim is to cash out before the aircraft flies away and the round ends.

When playing with real money, a successful return is calculated by multiplying the selected stake by the multiplier shown at the time of cash-out. If the aircraft flies away before the player cashes out, the stake placed on that round is lost.

In the Aviatrix demo, this process is experienced using virtual funds instead of real Ghana cedis. This provides a convenient way to become familiar with the game before deciding whether to play for real.

Understanding the Aviatrix Multiplier

The multiplier begins at the start of a round and increases while the aircraft remains in flight. The longer the round continues, the higher the multiplier may rise. However, waiting for a higher multiplier also carries the risk that the aircraft will fly away before you cash out.

For example, if a real-money stake of GH₵10 is successfully cashed out at a 2.00x multiplier, the total return would be GH₵20. This includes the original GH₵10 stake and a GH₵10 win.
